
# 📊 automated-market-research-agent (AI-Powered Multi-Agent System)

An advanced **CrewAI-based multi-agent system** that performs end-to-end market research, competitor analysis, customer insights, product strategy, and business evaluation for any AI product idea.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This system simulates a real-world startup research team using multiple AI agents working sequentially to generate a complete **investment-grade business report**.

It analyzes any product idea and produces insights on:
- 📈 Market size & growth (TAM, SAM, SOM)
- 🏆 Competitive landscape
- 👥 Customer segmentation & personas
- 🧩 Product strategy & MVP planning
- 💰 Pricing & revenue model
- ⚠️ Risk analysis
- 📊 Final Go / No-Go decision

---

## 🧠 AI Agents

- Market Research Specialist  
- Competitive Intelligence Analyst  
- Customer Insights Researcher  
- Product Strategy Advisor  
- Business Analyst  

Each agent is specialized with:
- Defined role  
- Clear goals  
- Industry expertise  
- Web research capabilities  

---

## ⚙️ Tech Stack

- Python 🐍  
- CrewAI 🤖  
- SerperDevTool 🌐 (Search API)  
- ScrapeWebsiteTool 🧹  
- SeleniumScrapingTool 🕷  
- OpenAI / LLMs 🧠  
- Markdown Report Generation 📄  

---

## 🔄 Workflow

1. User provides a **product idea**
2. Market Research Agent analyzes industry & trends
3. Competitive Intelligence Agent studies competitors
4. Customer Insights Agent builds personas & needs
5. Product Strategy Agent designs MVP & roadmap
6. Business Analyst generates final investment report
